How it works

This isn't a cartoon — it's a real solver. Every wire, resistor, bulb and battery becomes an edge in a circuit graph, and PhysicsLab solves Kirchhoff's Current & Voltage Laws simultaneously (Modified Nodal Analysis) to find the exact voltage at every node and current through every part.

Moving dots show real current — direction and speed reflect the solved values. Bulbs glow brighter with more power (P = I²R).
